# What is Leitstelle?
A firefighting game in which you're both: the dispatcher in the control centre and the incident commander on scene. The emergency call comes in at a real address, you alert the right vehicles from real stations, and when they arrive you take over yourself: dismount the crew, fetch the equipment, lay the hoses, set up the turntable ladder.

Fire and water are simulated. A fire grows when too little water reaches it, and you build the water supply yourself. You can play solo or in co-op with friends.
