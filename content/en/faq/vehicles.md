# Which vehicles are in the game?
The HLF 20, the DLK 23-12, the LF 8 and the TSF already drive with their own models. The MLF, TLF 3000, RW, ELW 1 and GW-L2 are in too, some of them still using a similar vehicle's model until their own is finished.

The stations get their real brigade's vehicles, as far as they fit into the game.
