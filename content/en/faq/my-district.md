# Is my district or my station in the game?
It starts with the district of Freising: all 83 fire stations, every mapped hydrant and the brigades' vehicles, built from OpenStreetMap and the Bavarian terrain model. The technology behind it is built for the whole of Germany and beyond, and more districts will follow.

If you'd like to see your station in the game, tell us about it in the [forum](https://forum.leitstelle.io). Photos and vehicle lists help enormously.
