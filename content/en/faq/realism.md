# How realistic is the game?
As real as possible where it matters: real alarm keywords (B 1–4, THL 1–3), the vehicles' FMS radio status, real stations and vehicles, and nothing happens on scene by itself. I'm a trained industrial firefighter myself and check every detail against reality.

To be honest, the game simplifies too: crews are smaller than in real life, so an incident stays manageable. If something looks wrong to you, say so in the [forum](https://forum.leitstelle.io).
